5Brief part 2 — new tokens, measured · the busy-day composite · what competes
Relationships decided before drawing
  • Health strip is secondary; chips stay the filter and the only live count. The strip prints nothing a chip already prints. It shows the day's outcome — stops done, on time vs late — which no chip can express, because an Arrived chip counts trucks on site now, not deliveries made.
  • Multi-select is checkboxes in a 28px leading column; shift-click on a checkbox extends a range. Plain shift-click on rows was rejected: row click already means select + map follow, and ↑↓ own selection.
  • Trail is selected-only — your assumption holds. 30 trails at once would read as traffic, not history.
  • ⌘K is navigation plus view-only actions (pin, density, apply a saved view, open shortcuts). No status writes: a write from the palette would skip the reason and expiry form.
  • ? is matched on the key value, not the physical key — on US layouts it shares a key with /. Suppressed inside inputs, textareas and contenteditable, same guard as every single-key binding.

  • No codebase is connected, so the keymap can't be pulled from source. The cheat sheet will list the 2g keymap (/ Esc ↑↓ Enter E 1–7 0) plus the new bindings proposed here: ? ⌘K P X D. Connect the repo and I'll diff against it.
  • I don't have docs/design-spec.md; 2g + 3e are the spec being extended.
  • The brief says twelve and lists fifteen. All fifteen are in scope.
  • Found while measuring: muted ink inside every existing modal (2c 3a 4a 4c) is #858d94 on #252a30 — 4.29:1, a fail. New token below; I'll apply it retroactively in the next pass.

5cWhat competed in 5b, and the rule each collision produced
Flash vs checked ground
Both tint the row. Flash wins for its 1.6s, then the row settles to row.checked. Checked state is carried by the tick, so nothing is lost while the ground is borrowed.
Re-sort under the cursor
A flip re-sorts the row, and with a flash that is legible. But while the pointer is over the list or 2+ rows are checked, re-sort is held (max 10s) so a click never lands on a row that just moved. The flash and toast still fire in place.
Bulk bar vs fold footer
Same 44px slot. The bar replaces the footer while 2+ are checked and keeps the one fact that mattered — the problems-below-the-fold count — at its right end.
Pinned vs urgency groups
Above 40 trucks (3d) the pinned block sits above the first group header. Pinned trucks are removed from their group, not duplicated, and the group count reads "Late 4 · +1 pinned". Cap of 5 so the block never pushes the problem set below the fold.
Pin vs checkbox vs copy
Three per-row affordances, three different cells: checkbox in its own column, pin after the truck number, copy at the right edge of Next stop and the load cell on hover only. None shares a hit area; none widens a fixed column.
Search field vs ⌘K
Two text boxes that both find trucks. Split by verb: / filters the list in place, ⌘K jumps and closes. The field's placeholder now says "Filter list…" and shows both hints.
Health strip vs chips
No number appears in both. Chips: trucks by state now. Strip: stops done today, split on time / late. Placed in the list toolbar, not the header, so it's read with the list rather than competing with the chip row.
Trail vs markers
Dots, no stroke, 4–5px — smaller than any marker (14px+) and never a line, so a future route preview can own the solid line. The selected marker gains a steel edge so trail and head read as one object.
Toast placement
Moved to the map's top-right: bottom-left now belongs to the bulk bar, and the list's top holds the pinned block.
Overlays
Palette, cheat sheet and tour share one scrim and one layer — only one open at a time. ⌘K during the tour ends the tour; ? inside the palette types a "?".

Where VL-000000 came from — answering item 6
I invented it. Nothing you sent specified a load-number format; I wrote a plausible-looking rule to demonstrate the field-level error pattern and it hardened into a spec. So the format check is dropped — 4c validates only that the field is non-empty and trims whitespace, which is the rule that survives a broker-supplied number in any shape. The error pattern itself is unchanged; it now demonstrates on an empty required field, which is the case that actually exists. If Vin does enforce an internal format, say so and I will reinstate it as a warning rather than a block — a broker's number that doesn't match yours is still the number on the rate con.

  • Own status? No new hue. Unassigned joins the data-integrity family and reuses its exact pair — #b3bac0 on #262a2f — separated from No appt and Stale GPS the way the 1a system already separates those two: border style, icon, marker pattern. 3Revisions — buildable pass
Answers to the two judgement calls
  • Reassignment stays in the modal (3a). At 20–60 trucks it is one select, and the common edit is a typo in a trailer number — a separate screen would make that a two-screen trip. What it gets instead is a guard: changing the assigned driver turns the footer into a named confirm, so it can't happen as a side effect of fixing the trailer.
  • The "no scroll" promise is retired (3c, 3d). It only ever held at ≤20 trucks on a 1040px-tall viewport — 21 rows at 44px is 924px of a 922px body. The replacement contract is stated on the screens: the problem set is always above the fold, and anything below the fold is counted in the footer bar by status, so a dispatcher never has to scroll to find out whether scrolling matters.
Corrections applied everywhere, not just here
  • Micro type is 10.5px in turns 1, 2 and 3 — the old 9.5px is gone from the file.
  • Selected-row ground moved down to #1b222b, not up, plus a dedicated muted token. Numbers in 3e.
  • Unchanged as instructed: every colour token, the marker shape system, the truncation order, the offline rule.

1Step one — status tokens & the truck row
What I locked from your answers
  • Primary canvas 2560×1440; ultrawide 3440 and MacBook 16 (1512) are graceful variants, phone gets full parity as a separate stack.
  • All times in dispatcher-local, abbreviation always printed. Stop-local shown in the popup and modal as a second line.
  • Row carries projected ETA as the seventh field.
  • ~20 trucks a shift, so the list is never virtualised and the panel can show the whole fleet without scroll at 1440.
  • Brand mark lives in the header only; the working UI is neutral.
Decisions I made for you — say if wrong
  • At risk = projected ETA lands inside 45 min of the appointment, or after it by less than 30 min, or the HOS clock can't cover remaining drive. Late = projected ETA past the appointment by 30 min or more, or the appointment has passed with no arrival.
  • Brand vs status: logo navy #0d2b6b is unusable as UI ink on dark (1.3:1). Interactive accent is the Industry steel at #94bce3 (9.0:1); logo cyan #29b6d8 appears in the mark and login only. No brand color touches red/amber/green.
  • Stale GPS and No appointment are data-integrity states, not schedule states — both render neutral grey, separated from each other by border style, icon and marker pattern, never hue.
